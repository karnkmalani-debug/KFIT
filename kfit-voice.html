📖 How to use Voice Input ▼
✅ What you CAN do
Upper
One exercise at a time or multiple in one go
"Goblet squat 3 sets 10 reps 12.5 kg" "Bench press 3 sets then Romanian deadlift 3 sets 10 reps 20kg"
📊
Different reps and weight per set
"Bench press set 1 ten reps 60kg set 2 eight reps 65kg set 3 six reps 70kg"
✏️
Edit anything after AI parses it — tap any field to correct the name, reps, or weight before saving
🗑️
Remove exercises from the parsed list before saving if something was picked up incorrectly
🔄
Re-speak if the AI misheard — tap the 🔄 button to try again with the same mic session
💡 Tips for best results
🗣️
Speak clearly and at a normal pace — don't rush. The mic stays open until you stop talking.
🔢
Say numbers as words or digits — both work
"ten reps" or "10 reps" — both fine
🏋️
Use the full exercise name — the more specific the better
"Romanian deadlift" not just "deadlift" "Lying hamstring curl" not just "curls"
📍
Quiet environment helps — background gym noise can cause mis-transcriptions
📅
Check the date before saving — defaults to today but you can change it for past sessions
❌ What you CANNOT do
⏱️
Log rest times or session duration by voice — these need to be tracked live in the main tracker
🏃
Log cardio activities by voice — treadmill, cycling etc. must be added in the tracker directly
📝
Add session notes — notes section is not available in voice mode
🌐
Works on Chrome and Safari only — some browsers don't support the microphone API
📵
Does not work offline — needs internet to send speech to AI for parsing
🔄 How it connects to your tracker
1️⃣
Speak your workout → AI parses it → you review and correct
2️⃣
Tap Save to KFit → saved directly to your account on Supabase
3️⃣
Open your KFit Fitness Tracker → session appears automatically in History
⚠️
Sign in with the same name and mobile as your KFit tracker — otherwise the session won't link to your account
