Train Smart. Age Well.
"The goal is not just to look good -- it is to stay vertical and upright at every age."
How to Train Every Rep
01
Slow Eccentric
Control the descent -- 2 to 4 seconds, every rep.
02
Pause at Full Range
A brief pause at the stretched position -- no bouncing out of it.
03
Fast Concentric
Maximum intent lifting the weight -- explosive, not rushed.
04
Full Range of Motion, Always
If you can't complete full range with a weight, reduce the load.
